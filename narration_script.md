# Innopolis Events — Presentation Script
## ~3-4 minutes total

---

### Slide 1 — Title (10 sec)
"Hi, I'm Maman Torebaev. Today I'm presenting Innopolis Events — a Telegram Mini App that lets students discover, create, and join events right on the campus map."

---

### Slide 2 — Problem & Solution (30 sec)
"So what's the problem? Right now, events at Innopolis are scattered across dozens of Telegram chats. There's no single place to see what's happening on campus, no map, no registration system.

Innopolis Events solves this. It's a Telegram Mini App — meaning it opens right inside Telegram, no installation needed. You get an interactive map with all events pinned, one-tap registration, and full Russian and English support."

---

### Slide 3 — Bot & Map Flow (30 sec)
"Here's how it works. You open our Telegram bot and send /start. The bot shows you buttons — Open Map, Profile, My Events, Signed Events, and Language.

When you tap Open Map, a Mini App opens with a Leaflet map centered on Innopolis. You can see all upcoming events as pins on the map. Tap any pin to see the event details — title, date, location, and how many people joined. From there you can register with one tap."

---

### Slide 4 — Create Event (30 sec)
"Creating an event is just as easy. Tap anywhere on the map, and a form slides up. You fill in the title, description, pick a category — we have nine: Education, Sport, Culture, Tech, Social, Food, Music, Gaming, and Other. 

Set the location name, date and time, and optionally a participant limit. The GPS coordinates are auto-filled from where you tapped. Hit Create — and your event instantly appears on the map for everyone to see."

---

### Slide 5 — My Events, Signed Events, Profile (25 sec)
"The app also tracks everything for you. My Events shows all events you created. Signed Events shows events you registered for. And the Profile page shows your stats — events created, events joined, member since date, and a language switcher between English and Russian.

As you can see, the app adapts to Telegram's dark theme automatically."

---

### Slide 6 — Key Features (20 sec)
"To summarize the key features: interactive map with search and category filters, native Telegram Mini App with automatic authentication, one-tap event registration with live participant count, nine event categories, user profiles synced from Telegram, and full bilingual support."

---

### Slide 7 — Tech Stack (25 sec)
"On the technical side — the frontend is Vue 3 with Vite, Tailwind CSS, and Leaflet for the map. State management with Pinia, internationalization with vue-i18n. The backend is Express.js with SQLite database. The Telegram bot uses the grammY framework. Everything is containerized with Docker and served through nginx with SSL."

---

### Slide 8 — Thank You (10 sec)
"Thank you! The project is open source on GitHub. Feel free to try the bot right now in Telegram."
